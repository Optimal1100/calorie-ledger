About you

Used to set your vitamin and mineral targets and to personalise the AI review. Stored on this device; sent to the AI only when you run a review or calculate targets with a key saved.

Daily targets

The split button sets protein, carbs and fat to 30%, 45% and 25% of your calorie goal.

Oura ring and device sync

This one repo does two jobs. Your Mac uploads your Oura summary to it every 2 hours. And every device where you enter the same repo and a read-and-write token keeps the same food log, targets and details, stored in the repo as ledger.json. API keys are never synced. The token stays on this device and is sent only to GitHub.

AI and food databases

Keys stay on this device and are sent only to the service they belong to. Get a free Gemini key at aistudio.google.com/apikey (no card needed). On the free tier, Google may use your photos to improve its products. Food search works without a USDA key, but the shared one only allows a handful of searches an hour; a free personal key from api.data.gov/signup allows 1,000.
