Oura and sync

How this works
A scheduled job in the repo fetches your Oura data every hour, and opening the app asks it for a fresh fetch. Every device that uses the same repo and a read-and-write token shares the same food log, weight, targets and details (stored as ledger.json). API keys are never synced. The token stays on this device and is only sent to GitHub.

AI and food data

About keys and privacy

Keys stay on this device and are only sent to the service they belong to. Get a free Gemini key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. Photos are sent only when you ask for an estimate and are not stored by this app.

A personal USDA key from api.data.gov/signup allows 1,000 searches an hour instead of a handful.
