Photo estimate evaluation

Runs the app's real photo pipeline (same prompt, model order, validation and database grounding) on your own photos and compares each result with a reference you trust: a weighed meal or a nutrition label. It uses the AI key saved in the app on this browser, and every photo costs one request. Results stay on this page; download the CSV to keep them.

For each photo, enter the reference calories and what was in it, then Run. After the run, tick whether the foods were identified correctly; that separates recognition errors from portion and nutrition-data errors.
